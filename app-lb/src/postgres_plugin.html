Lowercase letters, digits and _, starting with a letter. The database is created as ns_<namespace>_<name>. The secret holds url, host, port, database, user and password, ready for a deployment's env_from.

Deleting a database revokes its credential. Its data stays on the pool until an operator purges it, and its pg- secret stays until you delete it on the Secrets page.
