Naming conventions

CI runs in this namespace get ${{ secrets.KEY }} from three secrets, merged in this order with later ones winning:

cievery workflow and environment
ci.<workflow>one workflow, every environment
ci.<workflow>.<environment>one workflow in one environment (default unless the job names one)

In the workflow and environment parts, any character other than a letter, digit, - or _ becomes -. Secrets named pg-<name> were written by the Postgres plugin and hold a database's connection details.
